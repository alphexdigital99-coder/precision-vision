# Precision Vision

# RATHNAYAKA FITTERS — ADVANCED ANIMATION & INTERACTION BRIEF

## 1. Overall Animation Direction

Create a visually stunning, premium, bright and colourful website for Rathnayaka Fitters with sophisticated animations and interactive details.

The experience should combine:
- Modern optical technology and precision.
- A premium, light-coloured visual identity.
- Smooth, cinematic scrolling.
- Interactive lens-inspired visual effects.
- Professional B2B credibility.
- Engaging micro-interactions that encourage visitors to explore the website and submit enquiries.

**Important:** Animations must feel intentional, smooth and high-quality—not excessive, distracting or childish.

## 2. Hero Section — Make the First Impression Exceptional

Create a distinctive animated hero section with a white or soft pastel-blue background.

**Headline:** “Good Lenses. Great People. Better Vision.”

Animation ideas:
- Reveal the headline word by word or line by line using a smooth upward motion.
- Animate the supporting text with a subtle fade-in.
- Introduce the main laboratory image with a smooth mask reveal.
- Add floating, translucent lens shapes or soft optical rings behind the content.
- Use subtle moving light reflections across a lens-inspired graphic.
- Add small decorative dots, optical measurement marks and curved lines that move gently.
- Animate the main call-to-action buttons on entry.

The hero image should use authentic Rathnayaka Fitters laboratory photography wherever appropriate.

**Interactive hover:** Buttons should smoothly change colour, move upward by a few pixels and display a refined arrow animation.

Avoid heavy video backgrounds that make the page slow.

## 3. Scroll-Triggered Animations

As visitors scroll, reveal each section progressively.

- **Fade-up reveals:** Headings, paragraphs and cards appear smoothly.
- **Staggered reveals:** Product cards appear one after another.
- **Image mask reveals:** Laboratory photographs emerge through rounded or geometric masks.
- **Parallax effects:** Background optical patterns and selected images move at slightly different speeds.
- **Section transitions:** Soft pastel backgrounds transition naturally between sections.
- **Progress indicator:** A thin, subtle scroll-progress line can appear at the top of the page.

Keep animation distances short and timings consistent. Content should never remain invisible because an animation failed.

## 4. Interactive Product & Lens Cards

Create attractive, interactive cards for lens products, laboratory services and technologies.

On hover:
- Slightly lift the card.
- Add a soft, realistic shadow.
- Zoom the image gently.
- Reveal a short description or additional information.
- Animate a small arrow or “Explore” link.
- Change the card's pastel background or accent colour.

Use a consistent visual system with soft blue, mint, pale yellow and warm coral accents.

On mobile, make the same information available through tapping—never rely on hover alone.

## 5. Interactive Laboratory Gallery

Turn the real laboratory photographs into an engaging visual experience.

- Use an editorial image grid with different image sizes.
- Reveal images using smooth clipping or mask animations.
- Apply a restrained zoom effect when a visitor hovers over a photograph.
- Open larger images in an elegant lightbox when clicked.
- Add short captions identifying the actual equipment, workspace or process where verified.
- Include a smooth image-to-image transition in the gallery.

Use the uploaded laboratory photographs as the primary visual content. Do not use stock photographs in a way that implies they show the actual Rathnayaka Fitters facility.

## 6. Optical-Inspired Effects

Use creative effects that connect directly to the optical industry.

**Lens magnification effect:** Create a subtle magnification or refractive effect over a decorative lens graphic or a specifically designed image.

**Optical ring animation:** Use fine circular lines that slowly expand or rotate in the background.

**Light sweep:** Add a restrained reflection that travels across selected lens images.

**Precision-line animation:** Draw fine measurement lines and geometric outlines as sections enter the viewport.

**Lens comparison interaction:** Where appropriate, let visitors use a before-and-after slider to explore an educational lens illustration or coating demonstration.

These effects should be lightweight and should not distort actual product or laboratory photographs in ways that could mislead business customers.

## 7. Animated Statistics — Only With Verified Data

If Rathnayaka Fitters supplies verified business figures, display them in a clean statistics section with count-up animations.

Potential categories include years of operation, services offered or other genuine company metrics.

Do not invent numbers, experience claims, customer totals or production capacity. If verified figures are unavailable, replace this section with animated service highlights.

## 8. Smooth Navigation and Page Transitions

Implement:
- A sticky navigation bar that becomes slightly more compact while scrolling.
- A subtle background or shadow transition when the header changes state.
- Smooth anchor scrolling.
- An animated mobile navigation menu.
- Underline animations on navigation links.
- Elegant transitions between interactive states.
- A floating back-to-top control that appears after sufficient scrolling.

Keep navigation responsive, accessible and easy to use.

## 9. Animated Business Enquiry Section

Make the final enquiry section visually engaging without compromising its professional purpose.

Use a colourful pastel background, a subtle animated optical pattern and a clear headline such as:

“Let's Bring Better Vision to Your Business.”

Include a prominent “Contact Our Team” or “Make a Business Enquiry” button.

On interaction:
- Buttons respond with smooth feedback.
- Form fields show clear focus states.
- Validation messages appear gently.
- Submission displays a clear success or error state.

The form must actually work with the configured backend or submission service. Do not display a fake success message.

## 10. Technical Implementation

Use the existing project's technology stack where possible. If the project is being built from scratch, use Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.

Recommended animation tools:
- **Framer Motion / Motion:** Page elements, staggered reveals, hover interactions, menus and scroll-based animation.
- **CSS transitions and keyframes:** Lightweight effects, animated underlines, floating shapes and button interactions.
- **GSAP:** Optional, for complex, carefully controlled scroll sequences or advanced timelines.
- **React Lenis or equivalent:** Optional smooth scrolling, only if it improves the experience and remains accessible.

Do not install multiple animation libraries for the same purpose without a clear reason.

## 11. Performance and Accessibility

- Target smooth performance on ordinary mobile phones and desktop computers.
- Prefer transform and opacity animations.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.
- Avoid unnecessary continuous animation and excessive JavaScript.
- Respect the user's reduced-motion preference.
- Ensure all essential information remains visible if animation is disabled.
- Avoid scroll-jacking and forced scrolling.
- Maintain readable text, keyboard accessibility and sufficient colour contrast.
- Test animations on mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Ensure that animation never interferes with forms, navigation or business enquiries.

## 12. Final Quality Standard

The finished website must feel like a custom-designed, premium optical technology brand—not a generic template with animations added afterward.

Use a carefully coordinated combination of:
1. A memorable animated hero.
2. Smooth scroll-triggered reveals.
3. Interactive product and service cards.
4. An engaging real-photo laboratory gallery.
5. Optical-inspired visual effects.
6. Refined navigation and button interactions.
7. A polished, animated enquiry experience.

Build and test the actual working animations. Do not merely describe them, use static placeholders, or leave interaction elements unfinished.
